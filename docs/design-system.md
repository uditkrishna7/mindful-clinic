# Brand Voice

Professional but human.

Compassionate without sounding overly emotional.

Scientific without sounding academic.

Confident without sounding arrogant.

Clear and reassuring.

Every sentence should feel supportive, evidence-based, and easy to understand.

# Photography

Natural lighting.

Neutral clothing.

Simple background.

Authentic expressions.

No stock photos.

No staged therapy scenes.

Minimal editing.

# Buttons

One primary action per section.

Primary button:
Soft green background.

Secondary button:
White background with subtle border.

Avoid more than two CTA buttons on a screen.

# Spacing

Whitespace is part of the design.

Never crowd content.

Every section should feel breathable.

Readable line lengths.

Comfort over density.

# Motion Principles

Animations should guide attention.

Never distract.

Use fade, slide and scale.

Avoid bounce effects.

Respect reduced motion preferences.

# Components

Every component should be:

Reusable

Minimal

Accessible

Easy to understand

Easy to maintain

No duplicated UI.

# User Experience Goals

Within the first 10 seconds visitors should understand:

• Who Manvi is

• What she specializes in

• Why they can trust her

• How to contact her

The website should reduce cognitive load and make information easy to discover.
